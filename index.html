Umpty Dumpty

The raw half-note sway with no subdivision. Feel time only from beat to beat and you'll sound stupid — this is where bad time lives. Every pocket below is a way of telling a story inside this pulse.

Method: one slow pulse, many subdivisions. Practice tempos in octaves — 60 · 120 · 240 drink from the same well. Ref tracks: Wes Montgomery · SRV · Ray Charles · Pat Martino
